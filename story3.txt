THE MONKEY WHO WANTED TO BE A TEACHER

Momo was a monkey who loved giving advice.

"Eat your bananas!"
"Climb carefully!"
"Never sleep before lunch!"

One day, Momo saw Professor Owl teaching at the jungle school.

"I can do that!" he said.

When Professor Owl left the classroom, Momo jumped onto the teacher's chair.

"Good morning, students! Today I am your teacher!"

The animals clapped.

Momo wrote on the board:

HOW MANY BANANAS ARE THERE IN A BUNCH?

A rabbit answered, "Ten?"

"Wrong!"

"Twenty?"

"Wrong!"

"Then what is the answer?"

Momo scratched his head.

"It depends on how hungry I am."

Everyone laughed.

Momo decided to teach science.

"Today we will learn about gravity!"

He dropped a banana.

THUMP!

It landed on his foot.

"Ouch!"

The students laughed.

Momo then jumped from the table toward a tree branch.

He missed and landed in a bucket of water.

SPLASH!

Professor Owl returned.

"What happened?"

Momo climbed out and said, "I was demonstrating gravity."

"And what did you learn?" asked Professor Owl.

Momo smiled.

"Gravity works very well!"

Everyone laughed.

From that day on, Momo became a student instead of a teacher.

But every morning he still gave one piece of advice:

"Always check where the bucket is before you jump!"

THE END

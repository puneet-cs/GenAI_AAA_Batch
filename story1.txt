THE LAZY ROBOT WHO HATED HOMEWORK

Robo was a small robot who lived in a school. He was brilliant at mathematics and science, but he hated homework.

Every afternoon his teacher said, "Robo, don't forget your homework."

"Of course!" Robo replied.

Then he went home and hid under his blanket.

One day, Robo invented a homework machine using a calculator, a printer, three wires, and an old toaster.

"Perfect!" he shouted.

He pressed the button.

BEEP!

A paper came out:

"HOMEWORK: PLEASE DO IT YOURSELF."

Robo stared at it.

"Who programmed you?"

Another paper appeared:

"YOUR TEACHER."

The next morning, the teacher asked, "Where is your homework?"

"My machine ate it," Robo replied.

"Really? Then bring your machine to school tomorrow."

Robo froze.

That evening he opened the machine and found a note inside:

"Nice try!"

Robo laughed. From that day on, he did his homework.

But he invented one more machine: a machine that made hot chocolate whenever he finished a homework question.

Suddenly, Robo became the fastest student in class!

THE END

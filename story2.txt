THE DOG WHO WANTED TO BE A SCIENTIST

Bruno was a clever dog who loved science. Every morning he wore a tiny white coat and entered his laboratory.

His first experiment was to discover which food dogs liked most.

Plate One: Carrots.
Plate Two: Biscuits.
Plate Three: Chicken.

Bruno carefully observed all three plates.

Then he ate everything.

His assistant, a cat named Mimi, asked, "What did you discover?"

"Chicken disappears the fastest," Bruno said.

The next day Bruno invented a machine that could throw a ball automatically.

WHOOSH!

The ball flew across the room.

Bruno chased it and brought it back.

WHOOSH!

Again!

After twenty minutes, Bruno was exhausted.

"Why don't you turn off the machine?" Mimi asked.

Bruno replied, "Because I'm a scientist. I need more research!"

Suddenly the machine went crazy.

WHOOSH! WHOOSH! WHOOSH!

Balls flew everywhere. One landed on Bruno's head.

BONK!

Mimi laughed.

Bruno proudly announced, "I have discovered another important scientific fact."

"What?"

"Science is dangerous when your ball machine has too much energy."

From that day on, Bruno's laboratory had one rule:

NO BALLS WITHOUT SAFETY GOGGLES!

THE END
